G1 BSP Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

G1 is the parallel British Standard Pipe thread (BSPP) of nominal size 1: 11 threads per inch on a 55° Whitworth form with rounded crests and roots, and a major diameter of 33.249 mm (1.3090 in). The size names the bore of the pipe it was once cut on, so nothing on the thread measures 1 inch.

G1 is used for garden taps, pump unions and water meters.

G1 tap drill size

Tap drill for G1: 30.75 mm (1.2106 in)

This is 0.46 mm over the basic minor diameter of 30.292 mm and leaves about 85% thread.

ISO 228-1 puts the whole tolerance on the minor diameter of the internal thread above the basic size, so the hole is always drilled larger than 30.292 mm. Drilling to the basic minor diameter itself leaves the tap cutting on its full profile and risks breaking it.

G1 and the tapered R1 (BSPT)

The taper thread of ISO 7-1 (EN 10226), written R1 for the male and Rc1 or Rp1 for the female, has the same 11 threads per inch and the same 55° form as G1, and at its gauge plane it has the same diameters. It differs in being cut on a 1 in 16 taper and in sealing on the thread with a sealant. The male R1 mates with a taper Rc1 or a parallel Rp1 female thread. Rp is not the same thing as G: the basic sizes agree but the tolerances do not, so a G1 port is not a specified mate for a taper male. The tap drill for the taper internal thread Rc1 is 29.2 mm, and for the parallel internal thread Rp1 it is 30.25 mm; both are smaller than the 30.75 mm used for G1.

How G1 seals

A G1 joint does not seal on its threads. ISO 228-1 is the standard for pipe threads where the pressure-tight joint is made outside the thread: the male fitting clamps a bonded washer, an O-ring or a flat gasket against the face of the port. The thread only supplies the clamping force, so thread sealant is not what makes the joint tight.

G1 against 1 NPT

The American NPT thread of the same nominal size has 11.5 threads per inch against 11, a 60° flank angle and an outside diameter of 33.401 mm against 33.249 mm. The parts do not fit each other. Use an adapter.
