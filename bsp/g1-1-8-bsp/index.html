G1-1/8 BSP Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

G1-1/8 is the parallel British Standard Pipe thread (BSPP) of nominal size 1-1/8: 11 threads per inch on a 55° Whitworth form with rounded crests and roots, and a major diameter of 37.897 mm (1.4920 in). The size names the bore of the pipe it was once cut on, so nothing on the thread measures 1-1/8 inch.

G1-1/8 tap drill size

Tap drill for G1-1/8: 35.5 mm (1.3976 in)

This is 0.56 mm over the basic minor diameter of 34.940 mm and leaves about 81% thread.

ISO 228-1 puts the whole tolerance on the minor diameter of the internal thread above the basic size, so the hole is always drilled larger than 34.940 mm. Drilling to the basic minor diameter itself leaves the tap cutting on its full profile and risks breaking it.

Is there a tapered 1-1/8 BSP?

ISO 7-1, the standard for taper BSP threads (R, Rc and Rp), does not include the 1-1/8 size. G1-1/8 exists only as a parallel thread.

How G1-1/8 seals

A G1-1/8 joint does not seal on its threads. ISO 228-1 is the standard for pipe threads where the pressure-tight joint is made outside the thread: the male fitting clamps a bonded washer, an O-ring or a flat gasket against the face of the port. The thread only supplies the clamping force, so thread sealant is not what makes the joint tight.
