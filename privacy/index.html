Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

ThreadSpec.org is a free reference website. We keep data collection to a minimum — this page explains what is collected and why.

What We Collect

We do not require accounts, and we do not ask for names, email addresses, or any other personal details to use the site. The thread calculator runs entirely in your browser — values you enter are never sent to our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are useful and how visitors find the site. Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, approximate location (city level), browser and device type, and referring website. This is done using cookies and similar identifiers set by Google.

The analytics data we see is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can learn how Google processes this data at policies.google.com/privacy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which is what keeps this reference free to use. Google and its certified partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure those ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google My Ad Center, opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info/choices, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required, Google’s consent tools ask for your permission before personalised ads are shown.

Cookies

The cookies on this site are set by Google Analytics (names beginning with _ga), used to distinguish visitors and measure traffic, and by Google AdSense and its advertising partners, used to serve and measure ads. We set no cookies of our own. Blocking or deleting any of these cookies does not affect any site functionality.

Hosting and Server Logs

The site is served as static pages. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, requested URL, timestamp, user agent) for security and operational purposes. We do not combine these logs with any other data.

Third-Party Content

Pages load the Google Analytics script from a third-party CDN (googletagmanager.com) and the Google AdSense script from googlesyndication.com; ads themselves are served by Google and its partners. The site's stylesheet (Tailwind CSS) is self-hosted and loaded directly from this server. When your browser requests these files, the operator of that service receives your IP address as part of the normal request.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since we hold no personal data about visitors, such requests are typically best directed to Google for analytics and advertising data. For anything else, contact us and we will help.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at info@threadspec.org.
