M2×0.4 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M2×0.4 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 2 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M2. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 0.4 mm (63.5 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M2 as a first-choice diameter.

M2 screws hold laptop storage drives and small electronics assemblies together.

  • Tap drill1.6 mm77% thread
  • Pitch0.4 mm63.5 TPI
  • Major diameter2.000 mm0.0787 in
  • Pitch diameter1.740 mm0.0685 in
  • Minor diameter (nut)1.567 mm0.0617 in
  • Tensile stress area2.07 mm²0.00321 in²
  • Clearance hole2.4 mmmedium fit, ISO 273

M2×0.4 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 19 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.019 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M2×0.4 tap drill size

Tap drill for M2×0.4: 1.6 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 2 − 0.4 = 1.6 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 1.567 and 1.679 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M2×0.4

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M2

The clearance hole depends only on the 2 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 2.4 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

How the M2×0.4 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.4 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.346 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 2 − 0.260 = 1.740 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 2 − 0.433 = 1.567 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 2 − 0.491 = 1.509 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 1.625² = 2.07 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 4.18°
