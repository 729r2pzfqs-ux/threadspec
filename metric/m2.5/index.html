M2.5×0.45 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M2.5×0.45 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 2.5 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M2.5. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 0.45 mm (56.4 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M2.5 as a first-choice diameter.

M2.5 is the usual standoff thread for single-board computers and laptop internals.

  • Pitch0.45 mm56.4 TPI

M2.5×0.45 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 20 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.020 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M2.5×0.45 tap drill size

Tap drill for M2.5×0.45: 2.05 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 2.5 − 0.45 = 2.05 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 2.013 and 2.138 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M2.5×0.45

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M2.5

The clearance hole depends only on the 2.5 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 2.9 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M2.5×0.45. The nearest is #3-56 UNF, which is 0.01 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 0.454 mm against 0.450 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M2.5×0.45 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.45 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.390 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 2.5 − 0.292 = 2.208 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 2.5 − 0.487 = 2.013 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 2.5 − 0.552 = 1.948 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 2.078² = 3.39 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.71°
