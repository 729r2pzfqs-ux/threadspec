M24×3 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M24×3 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 24 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M24. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 3 mm (8.5 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M24 as a first-choice diameter. Fine-pitch versions of the same diameter are M24×2, M24×1.5 and M24×1.

M24 is a standard structural and anchor bolt size.

M24×3 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 48 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.048 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M24×3 tap drill size

Tap drill for M24×3: 21 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 24 − 3 = 21 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 20.752 and 21.252 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M24×3

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M24

The clearance hole depends only on the 24 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 26 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M24×3. The nearest is 1-8 UNC, which is 1.40 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 3.175 mm against 3.000 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M24×3 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 3 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 2.598 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 24 − 1.949 = 22.051 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 24 − 3.248 = 20.752 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 24 − 3.681 = 20.319 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 21.185² = 352.50 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.48°
