M3×0.5 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M3×0.5 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 3 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M3. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 0.5 mm (50.8 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M3 as a first-choice diameter. Fine-pitch versions of the same diameter are M3×0.35.

M3 is the common screw for PC fans, optical and 2.5 inch drives, 3D printer frames and small enclosures.

M3×0.5 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 20 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.020 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M3×0.5 tap drill size

Tap drill for M3×0.5: 2.5 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 3 − 0.5 = 2.5 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 2.459 and 2.599 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M3×0.5

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M3

The clearance hole depends only on the 3 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 3.4 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M3×0.5. The nearest is #4-48 UNF, which is 0.16 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.529 mm against 0.500 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

Identify a thread from measurements

How the M3×0.5 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.5 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.433 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 3 − 0.325 = 2.675 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 3 − 0.541 = 2.459 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 3 − 0.613 = 2.387 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 2.531² = 5.03 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.40°
