M3.5×0.6 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M3.5×0.6 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 3.5 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M3.5. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 0.6 mm (42.3 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M3.5 as a second-choice diameter.

  • Tap drill2.9 mm77% thread
  • Pitch0.6 mm42.3 TPI
  • Major diameter3.500 mm0.1378 in
  • Pitch diameter3.110 mm0.1225 in
  • Minor diameter (nut)2.850 mm0.1122 in
  • Tensile stress area6.78 mm²0.0105 in²
  • Clearance hole3.9 mmmedium fit, ISO 273

M3.5×0.6 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 21 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.021 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M3.5×0.6 tap drill size

Tap drill for M3.5×0.6: 2.9 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 3.5 − 0.6 = 2.9 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 2.850 and 3.010 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M3.5×0.6

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M3.5

The clearance hole depends only on the 3.5 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 3.9 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M3.5×0.6. The nearest is #6-40 UNF, which is 0.01 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 0.635 mm against 0.600 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M3.5×0.6 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.6 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.520 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 3.5 − 0.390 = 3.110 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 3.5 − 0.650 = 2.850 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 3.5 − 0.736 = 2.764 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 2.937² = 6.78 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.51°
