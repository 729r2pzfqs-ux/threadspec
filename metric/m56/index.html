M56×5.5 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M56×5.5 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 56 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M56. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 5.5 mm (4.6 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M56 as a first-choice diameter.

  • Pitch5.5 mm4.6 TPI
  • Major diameter56.000 mm2.2047 in
  • Pitch diameter52.428 mm2.0641 in
  • Minor diameter (nut)50.046 mm1.9703 in

M56×5.5 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 75 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.075 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M56×5.5 tap drill size

Tap drill for M56×5.5: 50.5 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 56 − 5.5 = 50.5 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 50.046 and 50.796 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M56×5.5

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M56

The clearance hole depends only on the 56 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 62 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M56×5.5 strength

ISO 898-1 covers coarse threads from M1.6 to M39, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M56×5.5. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 2030 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 1620 kN.

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M56×5.5. The nearest is 2-1/4-4.5 UNC, which is 1.15 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 5.644 mm against 5.500 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M56×5.5 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 5.5 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 4.763 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 56 − 3.572 = 52.428 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 56 − 5.954 = 50.046 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 56 − 6.748 = 49.252 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 50.840² = 2030.02 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.91°
