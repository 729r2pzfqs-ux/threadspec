M5×0.8 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M5×0.8 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 5 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M5. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 0.8 mm (31.7 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M5 as a first-choice diameter. Fine-pitch versions of the same diameter are M5×0.5.

M5 is the bicycle accessory thread (bottle cages, racks, mudguards) and a common size for 19 inch rack screws and aluminium T-slot profiles.

M5×0.8 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 24 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.024 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M5×0.8 tap drill size

Tap drill for M5×0.8: 4.2 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 5 − 0.8 = 4.2 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 4.134 and 4.334 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M5×0.8

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M5

The clearance hole depends only on the 5 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 5.5 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M5×0.8. The nearest is #10-32 UNF, which is 0.17 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.794 mm against 0.800 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M5×0.8 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.8 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.693 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 5 − 0.520 = 4.480 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 5 − 0.866 = 4.134 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 5 − 0.981 = 4.019 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 4.249² = 14.18 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.25°
