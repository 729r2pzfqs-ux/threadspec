M1.6×0.35 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M1.6×0.35 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 1.6 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M1.6. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 0.35 mm (72.6 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M1.6 as a first-choice diameter.

  • Tap drill1.25 mm77% thread
  • Pitch0.35 mm72.6 TPI
  • Major diameter1.600 mm0.0630 in
  • Pitch diameter1.373 mm0.0540 in
  • Minor diameter (nut)1.221 mm0.0481 in
  • Tensile stress area1.27 mm²0.00197 in²
  • Clearance hole1.8 mmmedium fit, ISO 273

M1.6×0.35 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 19 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.019 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M1.6×0.35 tap drill size

Tap drill for M1.6×0.35: 1.25 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 1.6 − 0.35 = 1.25 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 1.221 and 1.321 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M1.6×0.35

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M1.6

The clearance hole depends only on the 1.6 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 1.8 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M1.6×0.35. The nearest is #0-80 UNF, which is 0.08 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.318 mm against 0.350 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M1.6×0.35 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.35 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.303 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 1.6 − 0.227 = 1.373 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 1.6 − 0.379 = 1.221 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 1.6 − 0.429 = 1.171 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 1.272² = 1.27 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 4.64°
