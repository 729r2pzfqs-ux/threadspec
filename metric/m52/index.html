M52×5 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M52×5 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 52 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M52. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 5 mm (5.1 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M52 as a second-choice diameter.

M52×5 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 71 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.071 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M52×5 tap drill size

Tap drill for M52×5: 47 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 52 − 5 = 47 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 46.587 and 47.297 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M52×5

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M52

The clearance hole depends only on the 52 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 56 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M52×5 strength

ISO 898-1 covers coarse threads from M1.6 to M39, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M52×5. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 1760 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 1410 kN.

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M52×5. The nearest is 2-4.5 UNC, which is 1.20 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 5.644 mm against 5.000 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M52×5 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 5 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 4.330 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 52 − 3.248 = 48.752 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 52 − 5.413 = 46.587 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 52 − 6.134 = 45.866 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 47.309² = 1757.83 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.87°
