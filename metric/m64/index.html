M64×6 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M64×6 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 64 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M64. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 6 mm (4.2 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M64 as a first-choice diameter.

  • Tap drill58 mm77% thread
  • Pitch6 mm4.2 TPI
  • Major diameter64.000 mm2.5197 in
  • Pitch diameter60.103 mm2.3663 in
  • Minor diameter (nut)57.505 mm2.2640 in
  • Tensile stress area2680 mm²4.15 in²
  • Clearance hole70 mmmedium fit, ISO 273

M64×6 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 80 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.080 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M64×6 tap drill size

Tap drill for M64×6: 58 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 64 − 6 = 58 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 57.505 and 58.305 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M64×6

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M64

The clearance hole depends only on the 64 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 70 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M64×6 strength

ISO 898-1 covers coarse threads from M1.6 to M39, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M64×6. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 2680 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 2140 kN.

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M64×6. The nearest is 2-1/2-4 UNC, which is 0.50 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 6.350 mm against 6.000 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M64×6 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 6 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 5.196 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 64 − 3.897 = 60.103 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 64 − 6.495 = 57.505 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 64 − 7.361 = 56.639 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 58.371² = 2675.97 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.82°
