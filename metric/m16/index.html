M16×2 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M16×2 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 16 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M16. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 2 mm (12.7 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M16 as a first-choice diameter. Fine-pitch versions of the same diameter are M16×1.5, M16×1.25 and M16×1.

M16 is a standard structural bolt size in steelwork and heavy machine frames.

M16×2 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 38 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.038 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M16×2 tap drill size

Tap drill for M16×2: 14 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 16 − 2 = 14 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 13.835 and 14.210 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M16×2

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M16

The clearance hole depends only on the 16 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 17.5 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M16×2. The nearest is 5/8-11 UNC, which is 0.12 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 2.309 mm against 2.000 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M16×2 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 2 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 1.732 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 16 − 1.299 = 14.701 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 16 − 2.165 = 13.835 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 16 − 2.454 = 13.546 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 14.124² = 156.67 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.48°
