M1.2×0.25 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M1.2×0.25 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 1.2 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M1.2. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 0.25 mm (101.6 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M1.2 as a first-choice diameter.

  • Pitch0.25 mm101.6 TPI

M1.2×0.25 tolerance limits

ISO 965-1 defines no grade 6 tolerances for a 0.25 mm pitch, so there are no 6g/6H limits for M1.2×0.25. ISO 965-2 specifies tolerance classes 5H for the nut and 6h for the screw on M1, M1.2 and M1.4.

M1.2×0.25 tap drill size

Tap drill for M1.2×0.25: 0.95 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 1.2 − 0.25 = 0.95 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

Other drills that work for M1.2×0.25

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M1.2

The clearance hole depends only on the 1.2 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 1.4 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M1.2×0.25 strength

ISO 898-1 covers coarse threads from M1.6 to M39, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M1.2×0.25. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 0.732 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 0.586 kN.

How the M1.2×0.25 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.25 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.217 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 1.2 − 0.162 = 1.038 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 1.2 − 0.271 = 0.929 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 1.2 − 0.307 = 0.893 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 0.965² = 0.73 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 4.39°
