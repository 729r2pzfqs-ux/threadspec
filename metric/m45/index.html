M45×4.5 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M45×4.5 is the ISO metric coarse thread for 45 mm fasteners, which is why it is usually written simply as M45. It has a 60° profile with a pitch of 4.5 mm (5.6 threads per inch). ISO 261 lists M45 as a second-choice diameter. Fine-pitch versions of the same diameter are M45×3, M45×2 and M45×1.5.

M45×4.5 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 63 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.063 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M45×4.5 tap drill size

Tap drill for M45×4.5: 40.5 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 45 − 4.5 = 40.5 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 40.129 and 40.799 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M45×4.5

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M45

The clearance hole depends only on the 45 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 48 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M45×4.5 strength

ISO 898-1 covers coarse threads from M1.6 to M39, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M45×4.5. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 1310 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 1050 kN.

All standard pitches on the M45 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M45×4.5. The nearest is 1-3/4-5 UNC, which is 0.55 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 5.080 mm against 4.500 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M45×4.5 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 4.5 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 3.897 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 45 − 2.923 = 42.077 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 45 − 4.871 = 40.129 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 45 − 5.521 = 39.479 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 40.778² = 1306.00 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.95°
