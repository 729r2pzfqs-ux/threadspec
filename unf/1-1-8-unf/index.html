1-1/8-12 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

1-1/8-12 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the 1-1/8 in (28.57 mm) diameter: 12 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.08333 in (2.117 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse 1-1/8-7 UNC it has 12 threads per inch instead of 7, a stress area 12% larger and a lead angle of 1.42° instead of 2.52°.

1-1/8-12 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0018 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

1-1/8-12 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for 1-1/8-12 UNF: 1-3/64" (1.0469 in, 26.59 mm)

This leaves about 72% thread in the tapped hole.

1-3/64" is the drill listed for 1-1/8-12 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0781 in under the 1.1250 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 1.035 and 1.053 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 1-1/8-12 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 1-1/8-12 UNF. The nearest is M30×2, which is 1.43 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 2.000 mm against 2.117 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 1-1/8-12 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.08333 in = 1 ÷ 12 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0722 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 1.1250 − 0.0541 = 1.0709 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 1.1250 − 0.0902 = 1.0348 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 1.1250 − 0.0992 = 1.0258 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 1.0438² = 0.85572 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.42°
