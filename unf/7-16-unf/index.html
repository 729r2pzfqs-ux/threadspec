7/16-20 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

7/16-20 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the 7/16 in (11.11 mm) diameter: 20 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.05000 in (1.270 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse 7/16-14 UNC it has 20 threads per inch instead of 14, a stress area 12% larger and a lead angle of 2.25° instead of 3.33°.

7/16-20 is a wheel stud thread on older and smaller American cars and the straight thread of the SAE -4 O-ring port.

7/16-20 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0013 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

7/16-20 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for 7/16-20 UNF: 25/64" (0.3906 in, 9.92 mm)

This leaves about 72% thread in the tapped hole.

25/64" is the drill listed for 7/16-20 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0469 in under the 0.4375 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.383 and 0.395 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 7/16-20 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

How the 7/16-20 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.05000 in = 1 ÷ 20 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0433 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.4375 − 0.0325 = 0.4050 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.4375 − 0.0541 = 0.3834 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.4375 − 0.0595 = 0.3780 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.3888² = 0.11872 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.25°
