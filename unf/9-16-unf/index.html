9/16-18 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

9/16-18 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the 9/16 in (14.29 mm) diameter: 18 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.05556 in (1.411 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse 9/16-12 UNC it has 18 threads per inch instead of 12, a stress area 12% larger and a lead angle of 1.92° instead of 2.99°.

9/16-18 is the pedal thread on adult bicycles (left pedal left-handed) and the straight thread of the SAE -6 O-ring port.

9/16-18 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0014 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

9/16-18 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for 9/16-18 UNF: 33/64" (0.5156 in, 13.10 mm)

This leaves about 65% thread in the tapped hole.

33/64" is the drill listed for 9/16-18 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0469 in under the 0.5625 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.502 and 0.515 in. The charted drill is slightly under that band: the tap trims the crests to size, at the cost of higher tapping torque. A drill inside the band is the safer choice in tough materials.

Other drills that work for 9/16-18 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for 9/16 in

ASME B18.2.8 does not list the 9/16 in size, so there is no standard clearance hole for the 9/16 in size. Size the hole from the fit the joint needs.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 9/16-18 UNF. The nearest is M14×1.5, which is 0.29 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.500 mm against 1.411 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 9/16-18 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.05556 in = 1 ÷ 18 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0481 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.5625 − 0.0361 = 0.5264 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.5625 − 0.0601 = 0.5024 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.5625 − 0.0662 = 0.4963 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.5084² = 0.20298 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.92°
