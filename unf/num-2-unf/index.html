#2-64 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

#2-64 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the number 2 size (major diameter 0.086 in, 2.18 mm): 64 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.01562 in (0.397 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse #2-56 UNC it has 64 threads per inch instead of 56, a stress area 6% larger and a lead angle of 3.75° instead of 4.37°.

#2-64 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0006 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

#2-64 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for #2-64 UNF: #50 (0.0700 in, 1.78 mm)

This leaves about 79% thread in the tapped hole.

#50 is the drill listed for #2-64 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0160 in under the 0.0860 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.069 and 0.075 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for #2-64 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

#2-64 UNF strength

SAE J429 starts at 1/4 in, so the SAE grades give no proof load for #2-64 UNF. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 0.00394 in² by the strength of the material: for example, 60,000 psi gives 236 lbf.

#2 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the #2 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

How the #2-64 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.01562 in = 1 ÷ 64 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0135 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.0860 − 0.0101 = 0.0759 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.0860 − 0.0169 = 0.0691 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.0860 − 0.0186 = 0.0674 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.0708² = 0.00393 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.75°
