5/8-18 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

5/8-18 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the 5/8 in (15.88 mm) diameter: 18 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.05556 in (1.411 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse 5/8-11 UNC it has 18 threads per inch instead of 11, a stress area 13% larger and a lead angle of 1.72° instead of 2.93°.

  • Tap drill37/64"65% thread, 14.68 mm
  • Pitch18 TPI1.411 mm
  • Major diameter0.6250 in15.875 mm
  • Pitch diameter0.5889 in14.958 mm
  • Minor diameter (nut)0.5649 in14.347 mm
  • Tensile stress area0.256 in²165 mm²
  • Clearance hole11/16 innormal fit, ASME B18.2.8

5/8-18 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0014 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

5/8-18 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for 5/8-18 UNF: 37/64" (0.5781 in, 14.68 mm)

This leaves about 65% thread in the tapped hole.

37/64" is the drill listed for 5/8-18 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0469 in under the 0.6250 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.565 and 0.578 in. The charted drill is slightly under that band: the tap trims the crests to size, at the cost of higher tapping torque. A drill inside the band is the safer choice in tough materials.

Other drills that work for 5/8-18 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 5/8-18 UNF. The nearest is M16×1.5, which is 0.12 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.500 mm against 1.411 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 5/8-18 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.05556 in = 1 ÷ 18 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0481 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.6250 − 0.0361 = 0.5889 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.6250 − 0.0601 = 0.5649 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.6250 − 0.0662 = 0.5588 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.5709² = 0.25596 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.72°
