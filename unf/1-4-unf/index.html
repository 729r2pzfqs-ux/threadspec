1/4-28 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

1/4-28 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the 1/4 in (6.35 mm) diameter: 28 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.03571 in (0.907 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse 1/4-20 UNC it has 28 threads per inch instead of 20, a stress area 14% larger and a lead angle of 2.87° instead of 4.18°.

1/4-28 is used for grease fittings and small aircraft and automotive fasteners.

1/4-28 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0010 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

1/4-28 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for 1/4-28 UNF: #3 (0.2130 in, 5.41 mm)

This leaves about 80% thread in the tapped hole.

#3 is the drill listed for 1/4-28 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0370 in under the 0.2500 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.211 and 0.220 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 1/4-28 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 1/4-28 UNF. The nearest is M6×1, which is 0.35 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.000 mm against 0.907 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

Identify a thread from measurements

How the 1/4-28 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.03571 in = 1 ÷ 28 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0309 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.2500 − 0.0232 = 0.2268 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.2500 − 0.0387 = 0.2113 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.2500 − 0.0425 = 0.2075 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.2152² = 0.03637 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.87°
