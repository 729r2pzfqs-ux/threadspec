#5-44 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

#5-44 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the number 5 size (major diameter 0.125 in, 3.17 mm): 44 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.02273 in (0.577 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse #5-40 UNC it has 44 threads per inch instead of 40, a stress area 4% larger and a lead angle of 3.75° instead of 4.18°.

#5-44 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0007 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

#5-44 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for #5-44 UNF: #37 (0.1040 in, 2.64 mm)

This leaves about 71% thread in the tapped hole.

#37 is the drill listed for #5-44 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0210 in under the 0.1250 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.100 and 0.108 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for #5-44 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

#5-44 UNF strength

SAE J429 starts at 1/4 in, so the SAE grades give no proof load for #5-44 UNF. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 0.00830 in² by the strength of the material: for example, 60,000 psi gives 498 lbf.

#5 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the #5 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with #5-44 UNF. The nearest is M3×0.5, which is 0.17 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.500 mm against 0.577 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the #5-44 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.02273 in = 1 ÷ 44 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0197 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.1250 − 0.0148 = 0.1102 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.1250 − 0.0246 = 0.1004 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.1250 − 0.0271 = 0.0979 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.1029² = 0.00831 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.75°
