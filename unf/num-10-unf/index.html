#10-32 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

#10-32 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the number 10 size (major diameter 0.190 in, 4.83 mm): 32 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.03125 in (0.794 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse #10-24 UNC it has 32 threads per inch instead of 24, a stress area 14% larger and a lead angle of 3.35° instead of 4.65°.

#10-32 is the traditional 19 inch rack screw, the green grounding screw in electrical boxes and a standard thread for small pneumatic fittings.

#10-32 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0009 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

#10-32 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for #10-32 UNF: #21 (0.1590 in, 4.04 mm)

This leaves about 76% thread in the tapped hole.

#21 is the drill listed for #10-32 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0310 in under the 0.1900 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.156 and 0.164 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

#10-32 UNF strength

SAE J429 starts at 1/4 in, so the SAE grades give no proof load for #10-32 UNF. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 0.0200 in² by the strength of the material: for example, 60,000 psi gives 1,200 lbf.

#10 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the #10 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with #10-32 UNF. The nearest is M5×0.8, which is 0.17 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 0.800 mm against 0.794 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the #10-32 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.03125 in = 1 ÷ 32 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0271 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.1900 − 0.0203 = 0.1697 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.1900 − 0.0338 = 0.1562 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.1900 − 0.0372 = 0.1528 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.1596² = 0.01999 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.35°
