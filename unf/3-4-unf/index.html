3/4-16 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

3/4-16 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the 3/4 in (19.05 mm) diameter: 16 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.06250 in (1.587 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse 3/4-10 UNC it has 16 threads per inch instead of 10, a stress area 12% larger and a lead angle of 1.61° instead of 2.66°.

3/4-16 is the thread of the common spin-on oil filter and of the SAE -8 O-ring port.

3/4-16 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0015 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

3/4-16 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for 3/4-16 UNF: 11/16" (0.6875 in, 17.46 mm)

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

11/16" is the drill listed for 3/4-16 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0625 in under the 0.7500 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.682 and 0.696 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 3/4-16 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 3/4-16 UNF. The nearest is M20×1.5, which is 0.95 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.500 mm against 1.587 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 3/4-16 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.06250 in = 1 ÷ 16 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0541 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.7500 − 0.0406 = 0.7094 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.7500 − 0.0677 = 0.6823 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.7500 − 0.0744 = 0.6756 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.6891² = 0.37296 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.61°
