5/16-24 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

5/16-24 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the 5/16 in (7.94 mm) diameter: 24 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.04167 in (1.058 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series. Against the coarse 5/16-18 UNC it has 24 threads per inch instead of 18, a stress area 11% larger and a lead angle of 2.66° instead of 3.66°.

5/16-24 is used on automotive and aircraft fasteners where a fine thread is wanted.

5/16-24 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0011 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

5/16-24 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for 5/16-24 UNF: I (0.2720 in, 6.91 mm)

This leaves about 75% thread in the tapped hole.

I is the drill listed for 5/16-24 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0405 in under the 0.3125 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.267 and 0.277 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 5/16-24 UNF. The nearest is M8×1, which is 0.06 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.000 mm against 1.058 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 5/16-24 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.04167 in = 1 ÷ 24 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0361 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0271 = 0.2854 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0451 = 0.2674 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0496 = 0.2629 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.2719² = 0.05807 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.66°
