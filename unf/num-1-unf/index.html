#1-72 UNF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

#1-72 UNF is the Unified fine thread for the number 1 size (major diameter 0.073 in, 1.85 mm): 72 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.01389 in (0.353 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNF series as a secondary size. Against the coarse #1-64 UNC it has 72 threads per inch instead of 64, a stress area 6% larger and a lead angle of 3.95° instead of 4.52°.

#1-72 UNF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0006 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

#1-72 UNF tap drill size

Tap drill for #1-72 UNF: #53 (0.0595 in, 1.51 mm)

This leaves about 75% thread in the tapped hole.

#53 is the drill listed for #1-72 UNF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0135 in under the 0.0730 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.058 and 0.063 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for #1-72 UNF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

#1-72 UNF strength

SAE J429 starts at 1/4 in, so the SAE grades give no proof load for #1-72 UNF. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 0.00278 in² by the strength of the material: for example, 60,000 psi gives 167 lbf.

#1 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the #1 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with #1-72 UNF. The nearest is M1.8×0.35, which is 0.05 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.350 mm against 0.353 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the #1-72 UNF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.01389 in = 1 ÷ 72 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0120 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.0730 − 0.0090 = 0.0640 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.0730 − 0.0150 = 0.0580 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.0730 − 0.0165 = 0.0565 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.0595² = 0.00278 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.95°
