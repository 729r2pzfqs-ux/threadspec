13/16-20 UNEF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

13/16-20 UNEF is the Unified extra fine thread for the 13/16 in (20.64 mm) diameter: 20 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.05000 in (1.270 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNEF series as a secondary size.

  • Tap drill49/64"72% thread, 19.45 mm
  • Pitch20 TPI1.270 mm
  • Major diameter0.8125 in20.637 mm
  • Pitch diameter0.7800 in19.813 mm
  • Minor diameter (nut)0.7584 in19.263 mm
  • Tensile stress area0.458 in²295 mm²

13/16-20 UNEF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0013 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

13/16-20 UNEF tap drill size

Tap drill for 13/16-20 UNEF: 49/64" (0.7656 in, 19.45 mm)

This leaves about 72% thread in the tapped hole.

49/64" is the drill listed for 13/16-20 UNEF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0469 in under the 0.8125 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.758 and 0.770 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 13/16-20 UNEF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for 13/16 in

ASME B18.2.8 does not list the 13/16 in size, so there is no standard clearance hole for the 13/16 in size. Size the hole from the fit the joint needs.

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 13/16-20 UNEF. The nearest is M20×1.5, which is 0.64 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.500 mm against 1.270 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 13/16-20 UNEF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.05000 in = 1 ÷ 20 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0433 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.8125 − 0.0325 = 0.7800 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.8125 − 0.0541 = 0.7584 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.8125 − 0.0595 = 0.7530 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.7638² = 0.45818 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.17°
