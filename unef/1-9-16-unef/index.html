1-9/16-18 UNEF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

1-9/16-18 UNEF is the Unified extra fine thread for the 1-9/16 in (39.69 mm) diameter: 18 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.05556 in (1.411 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNEF series as a secondary size.

1-9/16-18 UNEF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0015 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

1-9/16-18 UNEF tap drill size

Tap drill for 1-9/16-18 UNEF: 1-33/64" (1.5156 in, 38.50 mm)

This leaves about 65% thread in the tapped hole.

Published charts rarely list this size. 1-33/64" is the nominal diameter minus 3/64 in, the same convention charts use for 9/16-18 and 5/8-18.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 1.502 and 1.515 in. The charted drill is slightly under that band: the tap trims the crests to size, at the cost of higher tapping torque. A drill inside the band is the safer choice in tough materials.

Other drills that work for 1-9/16-18 UNEF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for 1-9/16 in

ASME B18.2.8 covers sizes up to 1-1/2 in, so there is no standard clearance hole for the 1-9/16 in size. Size the hole from the fit the joint needs.

1-9/16-18 UNEF strength

SAE J429 stops at 1-1/2 in, so the SAE grades give no proof load for 1-9/16-18 UNEF. Bolts this large are made to specifications such as ASTM A307, A449 or A354, whose strengths depend on diameter. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 1.79 in² by the strength of the material: for example, 60,000 psi gives 107,000 lbf.

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 1-9/16-18 UNEF. The nearest is M39×1.5, which is 0.69 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.500 mm against 1.411 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 1-9/16-18 UNEF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.05556 in = 1 ÷ 18 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0481 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 1.5625 − 0.0361 = 1.5264 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 1.5625 − 0.0601 = 1.5024 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 1.5625 − 0.0662 = 1.4963 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 1.5084² = 1.78693 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 0.66°
