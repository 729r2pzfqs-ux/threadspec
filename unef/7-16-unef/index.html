7/16-28 UNEF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

7/16-28 UNEF is the Unified extra fine thread for the 7/16 in (11.11 mm) diameter: 28 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.03571 in (0.907 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNEF series. Against the coarse 7/16-14 UNC it has 28 threads per inch instead of 14, a stress area 20% larger and a lead angle of 1.57° instead of 3.33°.

  • Pitch28 TPI0.907 mm

7/16-28 UNEF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0011 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

7/16-28 UNEF tap drill size

Tap drill for 7/16-28 UNEF: Y (0.4040 in, 10.26 mm)

This leaves about 72% thread in the tapped hole.

Y is the drill listed for 7/16-28 UNEF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0335 in under the 0.4375 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.399 and 0.407 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 7/16-28 UNEF

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

How the 7/16-28 UNEF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.03571 in = 1 ÷ 28 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0309 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.4375 − 0.0232 = 0.4143 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.4375 − 0.0387 = 0.3988 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.4375 − 0.0425 = 0.3950 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.4027² = 0.12737 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.57°
