5/16-32 UNEF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

5/16-32 UNEF is the Unified extra fine thread for the 5/16 in (7.94 mm) diameter: 32 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.03125 in (0.794 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNEF series. Against the coarse 5/16-18 UNC it has 32 threads per inch instead of 18, a stress area 19% larger and a lead angle of 1.95° instead of 3.66°.

5/16-32 UNEF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0010 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

5/16-32 UNEF tap drill size

Tap drill for 5/16-32 UNEF: 9/32" (0.2812 in, 7.14 mm)

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

9/32" is the drill listed for 5/16-32 UNEF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0312 in under the 0.3125 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.279 and 0.286 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 5/16-32 UNEF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 5/16-32 UNEF. The nearest is M8×0.75, which is 0.06 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 0.750 mm against 0.794 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 5/16-32 UNEF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.03125 in = 1 ÷ 32 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0271 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0203 = 0.2922 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0338 = 0.2787 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0372 = 0.2753 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.2821² = 0.06248 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.95°
