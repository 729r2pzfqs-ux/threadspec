15/16-20 UNEF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

15/16-20 UNEF is the Unified extra fine thread for the 15/16 in (23.81 mm) diameter: 20 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.05000 in (1.270 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNEF series as a secondary size.

  • Tap drill57/64"72% thread, 22.62 mm
  • Pitch20 TPI1.270 mm
  • Major diameter0.9375 in23.812 mm
  • Pitch diameter0.9050 in22.988 mm
  • Minor diameter (nut)0.8834 in22.438 mm

15/16-20 UNEF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0014 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

15/16-20 UNEF tap drill size

Tap drill for 15/16-20 UNEF: 57/64" (0.8906 in, 22.62 mm)

This leaves about 72% thread in the tapped hole.

57/64" is the drill listed for 15/16-20 UNEF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0469 in under the 0.9375 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.883 and 0.895 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 15/16-20 UNEF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for 15/16 in

ASME B18.2.8 does not list the 15/16 in size, so there is no standard clearance hole for the 15/16 in size. Size the hole from the fit the joint needs.

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 15/16-20 UNEF. The nearest is M24×1.5, which is 0.19 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.500 mm against 1.270 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 15/16-20 UNEF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.05000 in = 1 ÷ 20 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0433 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.9375 − 0.0325 = 0.9050 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.9375 − 0.0541 = 0.8834 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.9375 − 0.0595 = 0.8780 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.8888² = 0.62042 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.01°
