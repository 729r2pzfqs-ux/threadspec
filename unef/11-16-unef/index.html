11/16-24 UNEF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

11/16-24 UNEF is the Unified extra fine thread for the 11/16 in (17.46 mm) diameter: 24 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.04167 in (1.058 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNEF series as a secondary size.

  • Tap drill41/64"87% thread, 16.27 mm
  • Pitch24 TPI1.058 mm
  • Major diameter0.6875 in17.462 mm
  • Pitch diameter0.6604 in16.775 mm
  • Minor diameter (nut)0.6424 in16.317 mm
  • Tensile stress area0.329 in²212 mm²

11/16-24 UNEF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0012 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

11/16-24 UNEF tap drill size

Tap drill for 11/16-24 UNEF: 41/64" (0.6406 in, 16.27 mm)

This leaves about 87% thread in the tapped hole.

41/64" is the drill listed for 11/16-24 UNEF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0469 in under the 0.6875 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.642 and 0.652 in. The charted drill is slightly under that band: the tap trims the crests to size, at the cost of higher tapping torque. A drill inside the band is the safer choice in tough materials.

Other drills that work for 11/16-24 UNEF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for 11/16 in

ASME B18.2.8 does not list the 11/16 in size, so there is no standard clearance hole for the 11/16 in size. Size the hole from the fit the joint needs.

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 11/16-24 UNEF. The nearest is M18×1, which is 0.54 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.000 mm against 1.058 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 11/16-24 UNEF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.04167 in = 1 ÷ 24 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0361 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.6875 − 0.0271 = 0.6604 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.6875 − 0.0451 = 0.6424 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.6875 − 0.0496 = 0.6379 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.6469² = 0.32868 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.15°
