5/8-24 UNEF Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

5/8-24 UNEF is the Unified extra fine thread for the 5/8 in (15.88 mm) diameter: 24 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.04167 in (1.058 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNEF series. Against the coarse 5/8-11 UNC it has 24 threads per inch instead of 11, a stress area 19% larger and a lead angle of 1.27° instead of 2.93°.

5/8-24 UNEF limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0012 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

5/8-24 UNEF tap drill size

Tap drill for 5/8-24 UNEF: 37/64" (0.5781 in, 14.68 mm)

This leaves about 87% thread in the tapped hole.

37/64" is the drill listed for 5/8-24 UNEF in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0469 in under the 0.6250 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.580 and 0.590 in. The charted drill is slightly under that band: the tap trims the crests to size, at the cost of higher tapping torque. A drill inside the band is the safer choice in tough materials.

Other drills that work for 5/8-24 UNEF

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 5/8-24 UNEF. The nearest is M16×1, which is 0.12 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.000 mm against 1.058 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 5/8-24 UNEF dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.04167 in = 1 ÷ 24 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0361 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.6250 − 0.0271 = 0.5979 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.6250 − 0.0451 = 0.5799 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.6250 − 0.0496 = 0.5754 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.5844² = 0.26824 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.27°
