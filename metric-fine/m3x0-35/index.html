M3×0.35 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M3×0.35 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 3 mm diameter with a 0.35 mm pitch (72.6 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 0.5 mm of M3×0.5. The thread is 0.08 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 5.03 to 5.61 mm² (11% more) and the lead angle falls from 3.40° to 2.30°.

M3×0.35 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 19 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.019 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M3×0.35 tap drill size

Tap drill for M3×0.35: 2.65 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 3 − 0.35 = 2.65 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 2.621 and 2.721 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M3×0.35

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M3

The clearance hole depends only on the 3 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 3.4 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M3×0.35 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M3×0.35. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 5.61 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 4.49 kN.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M3×0.35. The nearest is #4-48 UNF, which is 0.16 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.529 mm against 0.350 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M3×0.35 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.35 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.303 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 3 − 0.227 = 2.773 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 3 − 0.379 = 2.621 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 3 − 0.429 = 2.571 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 2.672² = 5.61 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.30°
