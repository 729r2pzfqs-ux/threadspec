M24×1.5 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M24×1.5 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 24 mm diameter with a 1.5 mm pitch (16.9 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 3 mm of M24×3. The thread is 0.81 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 353 to 401 mm² (14% more) and the lead angle falls from 2.48° to 1.19°.

  • Pitch1.5 mm16.9 TPI

M24×1.5 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 32 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.032 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M24×1.5 tap drill size

Tap drill for M24×1.5: 22.5 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 24 − 1.5 = 22.5 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 22.376 and 22.676 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M24×1.5

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M24

The clearance hole depends only on the 24 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 26 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M24×1.5 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M24×1.5. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 401 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 321 kN.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M24×1.5. The nearest is 15/16-20 UNEF, which is 0.19 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.270 mm against 1.500 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M24×1.5 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 1.5 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 1.299 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 24 − 0.974 = 23.026 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 24 − 1.624 = 22.376 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 24 − 1.840 = 22.160 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 22.593² = 400.89 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.19°
