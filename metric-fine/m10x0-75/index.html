M10×0.75 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M10×0.75 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 10 mm diameter with a 0.75 mm pitch (33.9 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 1.5 mm of M10×1.5. The thread is 0.41 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 58 to 67.9 mm² (17% more) and the lead angle falls from 3.03° to 1.44°.

M10×0.75 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 22 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.022 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M10×0.75 tap drill size

Tap drill for M10×0.75: 9.2 mm

This leaves about 82% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 10 − 0.75 = 9.25 mm. Tap drill tables round this to 9.2 mm, a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 9.188 and 9.378 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M10×0.75

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

M10×0.75 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M10×0.75. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 67.9 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 54.3 kN.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M10×0.75. The nearest is 3/8-32 UNEF, which is 0.48 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.794 mm against 0.750 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M10×0.75 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.75 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.650 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 10 − 0.487 = 9.513 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 10 − 0.812 = 9.188 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 10 − 0.920 = 9.080 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 9.296² = 67.88 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.44°
