M16×1.25 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M16×1.25 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 16 mm diameter with a 1.25 mm pitch (20.3 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 2 mm of M16×2. The thread is 0.41 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 157 to 173 mm² (10% more) and the lead angle falls from 2.48° to 1.50°.

M16×1.25 is not listed in ISO 261. The dimensions below follow from the ISO 68-1 profile, but taps, dies and fasteners in this pitch are special order. The standard fine pitches for M16 are 1.5 and 1 mm.

M16×1.25 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 28 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.028 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M16×1.25 tap drill size

Tap drill for M16×1.25: 14.8 mm

This leaves about 74% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 16 − 1.25 = 14.75 mm. Tap drill tables round this to 14.8 mm, a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 14.647 and 14.912 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M16×1.25

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M16

The clearance hole depends only on the 16 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 17.5 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M16×1.25 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M16×1.25. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 173 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 138 kN.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M16×1.25. The nearest is 5/8-18 UNF, which is 0.12 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.411 mm against 1.250 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M16×1.25 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 1.25 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 1.083 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 16 − 0.812 = 15.188 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 16 − 1.353 = 14.647 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 16 − 1.534 = 14.466 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 14.827² = 172.67 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.50°
