M5×0.5 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M5×0.5 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 5 mm diameter with a 0.5 mm pitch (50.8 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 0.8 mm of M5×0.8. The thread is 0.16 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 14.2 to 16.1 mm² (14% more) and the lead angle falls from 3.25° to 1.95°.

M5×0.5 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 20 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.020 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M5×0.5 tap drill size

Tap drill for M5×0.5: 4.5 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 5 − 0.5 = 4.5 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 4.459 and 4.599 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M5×0.5

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M5

The clearance hole depends only on the 5 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 5.5 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M5×0.5 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M5×0.5. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 16.1 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 12.9 kN.

M5 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the M5 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M5×0.5. The nearest is #10-32 UNF, which is 0.17 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.794 mm against 0.500 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M5×0.5 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.5 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.433 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 5 − 0.325 = 4.675 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 5 − 0.541 = 4.459 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 5 − 0.613 = 4.387 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 4.531² = 16.12 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.95°
