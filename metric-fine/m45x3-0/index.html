M45×3 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M45×3 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 45 mm diameter with a 3 mm pitch (8.5 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 4.5 mm of M45×4.5. The thread is 0.81 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 1310 to 1400 mm² (7% more) and the lead angle falls from 1.95° to 1.27°.

M45×3 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 48 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.048 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M45×3 tap drill size

Tap drill for M45×3: 42 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 45 − 3 = 42 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 41.752 and 42.252 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M45×3

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M45

The clearance hole depends only on the 45 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 48 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M45×3 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M45×3. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 1400 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 1120 kN.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M45×3. The nearest is 1-11/16-18 UNEF, which is 2.14 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.411 mm against 3.000 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M45×3 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 3 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 2.598 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 45 − 1.949 = 43.051 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 45 − 3.248 = 41.752 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 45 − 3.681 = 41.319 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 42.185² = 1397.70 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.27°
