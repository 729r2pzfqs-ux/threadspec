M22×2 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M22×2 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 22 mm diameter with a 2 mm pitch (12.7 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 2.5 mm of M22×2.5. The thread is 0.27 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 303 to 318 mm² (5% more) and the lead angle falls from 2.24° to 1.76°.

M22×2 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 38 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.038 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M22×2 tap drill size

Tap drill for M22×2: 20 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 22 − 2 = 20 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 19.835 and 20.210 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M22×2

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M22

The clearance hole depends only on the 22 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 24 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M22×2 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M22×2. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 318 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 254 kN.

M22 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the M22 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M22×2. The nearest is 7/8-14 UNF, which is 0.22 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.814 mm against 2.000 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M22×2 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 2 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 1.732 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 22 − 1.299 = 20.701 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 22 − 2.165 = 19.835 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 22 − 2.454 = 19.546 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 20.124² = 318.05 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.76°
