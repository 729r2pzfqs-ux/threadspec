M42×3 Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

M42×3 is an ISO metric fine-pitch thread: the 42 mm diameter with a 3 mm pitch (8.5 threads per inch) instead of the coarse 4.5 mm of M42×4.5. The thread is 0.81 mm shallower, so the tensile stress area rises from 1120 to 1210 mm² (8% more) and the lead angle falls from 2.10° to 1.37°.

M42×3 tolerance limits (6g bolt, 6H nut)

6g and 6H are the general-purpose tolerance classes for commercial screws and nuts. The 6g screw has a fundamental deviation of 48 µm, so even at its largest it sits 0.048 mm below the basic size and leaves room for a thin plating.

M42×3 tap drill size

Tap drill for M42×3: 39 mm

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

The rule for metric tap drills is diameter minus pitch: 42 − 3 = 39 mm, which is a stocked drill size.

The 6H minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 38.752 and 39.252 mm. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for M42×3

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for M42

The clearance hole depends only on the 42 mm diameter, not on the pitch. The medium series, 45 mm, is the usual choice for general assembly.

M42×3 strength

ISO 898-1 tabulates fine-pitch loads only for a selected set of threads from M8×1 to M39×3, so it gives no proof or breaking loads for M42×3. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 1210 mm² by the stress of the material: for example, 800 MPa gives 968 kN.

All standard pitches on the M42 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest inch thread

No inch thread is interchangeable with M42×3. The nearest is 1-5/8-18 UNEF, which is 0.73 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.411 mm against 3.000 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the M42×3 dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 3 mm
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 2.598 mm
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 42 − 1.949 = 40.051 mm
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 42 − 3.248 = 38.752 mm
  • d3 = d − 1.226869 × P = 42 − 3.681 = 38.319 mm (external root, 17H/24 deep)
  • As = π/4 × ((d2 + d3) ÷ 2)² = π/4 × 39.185² = 1205.98 mm²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 1.37°
