5-8 NPT Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

5 NPT, written in full as 5-8 NPT, is the American taper pipe thread for nominal 5 inch pipe: 8 threads per inch cut on a 1 in 16 taper, on pipe with an outside diameter of 5.563 in (141.30 mm). The nominal size names the pipe and is not a dimension you can measure on the thread.

Where the diameters are measured

A taper thread has no single pitch diameter. ASME B1.20.1 defines it at the end of the pipe (E0), at the plane where a fitting stops when screwed on by hand (E1), and at the end of the effective thread (E2). Each step of 1 in along the axis adds 1/16 in to the diameter.

5 NPT tap drill size

The hole for a taper tap is a compromise, because a straight drilled hole meets a tapered thread. The minor diameter of the thread is 5.2907 in at the small end (K0) and grows by 1/16 in per inch of depth.

ASME B1.20.1 notes that its suggested drills do not guarantee fully formed threads over the whole hand-tight length.

How far 5 NPT screws in

A 5 NPT fitting turns in by hand for 0.937 in (23.8 mm), about 7.5 threads, before the flanks meet. The standard then allows 2 more threads of wrench make-up, for a total engagement of about 1.187 in (30.1 mm). Tolerance on the hand-tight position is one thread either way, so a joint that stops a turn early or late is still within the standard.

NPT threads do not seal by themselves. The crests and roots leave a spiral gap, so ASME B1.20.1 calls for a sealant (PTFE tape or pipe compound) where the joint must be pressure-tight. Dryseal NPTF threads to ASME B1.20.3 have the same pitch and taper but controlled crests and roots that seal without it.
