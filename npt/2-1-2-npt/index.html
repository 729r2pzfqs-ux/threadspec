2-1/2-8 NPT Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

2-1/2 NPT, written in full as 2-1/2-8 NPT, is the American taper pipe thread for nominal 2-1/2 inch pipe: 8 threads per inch cut on a 1 in 16 taper, on pipe with an outside diameter of 2.875 in (73.02 mm). The nominal size names the pipe and is not a dimension you can measure on the thread.

Where the diameters are measured

A taper thread has no single pitch diameter. ASME B1.20.1 defines it at the end of the pipe (E0), at the plane where a fitting stops when screwed on by hand (E1), and at the end of the effective thread (E2). Each step of 1 in along the axis adds 1/16 in to the diameter.

The hole for a taper tap is a compromise, because a straight drilled hole meets a tapered thread. The minor diameter of the thread is 2.6195 in at the small end (K0) and grows by 1/16 in per inch of depth. Many printed charts give 2-5/8" for 2-1/2 NPT. That is 0.0156 in larger than the drill the standard suggests: it taps more easily but leaves shallower threads at the bottom of the hole. For the fullest thread, drill with the smaller size and follow with a 1 in 16 taper pipe reamer.

ASME B1.20.1 notes that its suggested drills do not guarantee fully formed threads over the whole hand-tight length.

How far 2-1/2 NPT screws in

A 2-1/2 NPT fitting turns in by hand for 0.682 in (17.3 mm), about 5.5 threads, before the flanks meet. The standard then allows 2 more threads of wrench make-up, for a total engagement of about 0.932 in (23.7 mm). Tolerance on the hand-tight position is one thread either way, so a joint that stops a turn early or late is still within the standard.

NPT threads do not seal by themselves. The crests and roots leave a spiral gap, so ASME B1.20.1 calls for a sealant (PTFE tape or pipe compound) where the joint must be pressure-tight. Dryseal NPTF threads to ASME B1.20.3 have the same pitch and taper but controlled crests and roots that seal without it.

2-1/2 NPT against G2-1/2 BSP

The British Standard Pipe thread of the same nominal size has a different pitch (11 against 8 threads per inch), a 55° flank angle instead of 60° and a major diameter of 75.184 mm against 73.025 mm. The two do not screw together properly. Use an adapter.
