5/16-18 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

5/16-18 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 5/16 in (7.94 mm) diameter: 18 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.05556 in (1.411 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 5/16-24 UNF and 5/16-32 UNEF.

5/16-18 is a common size for small engine, bracket and furniture bolts.

5/16-18 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0012 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

5/16-18 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 5/16-18 UNC: F (0.2570 in, 6.53 mm)

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

F is the drill listed for 5/16-18 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0555 in under the 0.3125 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.252 and 0.265 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 5/16-18 UNC

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 5/16-18 UNC. The nearest is M8×1.25, which is 0.06 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.250 mm against 1.411 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

Identify a thread from measurements

How the 5/16-18 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.05556 in = 1 ÷ 18 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0481 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0361 = 0.2764 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0601 = 0.2524 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.3125 − 0.0662 = 0.2463 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.2584² = 0.05243 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.66°
