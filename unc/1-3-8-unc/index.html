1-3/8-6 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

1-3/8-6 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 1-3/8 in (34.92 mm) diameter: 6 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.16667 in (4.233 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 1-3/8-12 UNF and 1-3/8-18 UNEF.

  • Pitch6 TPI4.233 mm
  • Major diameter1.3750 in34.925 mm

1-3/8-6 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0024 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

1-3/8-6 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 1-3/8-6 UNC: 1-7/32" (1.2188 in, 30.96 mm)

This leaves about 72% thread in the tapped hole.

1-7/32" is the drill listed for 1-3/8-6 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.1562 in under the 1.3750 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 1.195 and 1.225 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 1-3/8-6 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 1-3/8-6 UNC. The nearest is M36×4, which is 1.08 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 4.000 mm against 4.233 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 1-3/8-6 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.16667 in = 1 ÷ 6 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.1443 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 1.3750 − 0.1083 = 1.2667 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 1.3750 − 0.1804 = 1.1946 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 1.3750 − 0.1985 = 1.1765 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 1.2126² = 1.15488 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.40°
