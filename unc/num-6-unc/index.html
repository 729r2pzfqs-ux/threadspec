#6-32 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

#6-32 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the number 6 size (major diameter 0.138 in, 3.51 mm): 32 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.03125 in (0.794 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are #6-40 UNF.

#6-32 holds PC cases, power supplies and 3.5 inch drives, and fastens switches and receptacles to North American electrical boxes.

#6-32 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0008 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

#6-32 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for #6-32 UNC: #36 (0.1065 in, 2.71 mm)

This leaves about 78% thread in the tapped hole.

#36 is the drill listed for #6-32 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0315 in under the 0.1380 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.104 and 0.114 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for #6-32 UNC

#6-32 UNC strength

SAE J429 starts at 1/4 in, so the SAE grades give no proof load for #6-32 UNC. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 0.00909 in² by the strength of the material: for example, 60,000 psi gives 545 lbf.

#6 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the #6 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with #6-32 UNC. The nearest is M3.5×0.6, which is 0.01 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 0.600 mm against 0.794 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

Identify a thread from measurements

How the #6-32 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.03125 in = 1 ÷ 32 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0271 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.1380 − 0.0203 = 0.1177 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.1380 − 0.0338 = 0.1042 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.1380 − 0.0372 = 0.1008 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.1076² = 0.00909 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 4.83°
