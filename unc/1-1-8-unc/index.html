1-1/8-7 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

1-1/8-7 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 1-1/8 in (28.57 mm) diameter: 7 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.14286 in (3.629 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 1-1/8-12 UNF and 1-1/8-18 UNEF.

1-1/8-7 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0022 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

1-1/8-7 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 1-1/8-7 UNC: 63/64" (0.9844 in, 25.00 mm)

This leaves about 76% thread in the tapped hole.

63/64" is the drill listed for 1-1/8-7 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.1406 in under the 1.1250 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.970 and 0.998 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 1-1/8-7 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 1-1/8-7 UNC. The nearest is M30×3.5, which is 1.43 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 3.500 mm against 3.629 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 1-1/8-7 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.14286 in = 1 ÷ 7 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.1237 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 1.1250 − 0.0928 = 1.0322 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 1.1250 − 0.1546 = 0.9704 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 1.1250 − 0.1701 = 0.9549 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.9858² = 0.76328 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.52°
