3/8-16 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

3/8-16 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 3/8 in (9.52 mm) diameter: 16 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.06250 in (1.587 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 3/8-24 UNF and 3/8-32 UNEF.

3/8-16 is the larger tripod and lighting stand thread (ISO 1222) and a very common general-purpose bolt.

3/8-16 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0013 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

3/8-16 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 3/8-16 UNC: 5/16" (0.3125 in, 7.94 mm)

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

5/16" is the drill listed for 3/8-16 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0625 in under the 0.3750 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.307 and 0.321 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 3/8-16 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 3/8-16 UNC. The nearest is M10×1.5, which is 0.48 mm larger in diameter with a pitch of 1.500 mm against 1.587 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 3/8-16 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.06250 in = 1 ÷ 16 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0541 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.3750 − 0.0406 = 0.3344 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.3750 − 0.0677 = 0.3073 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.3750 − 0.0744 = 0.3006 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.3141² = 0.07749 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.40°
