7/8-9 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

7/8-9 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 7/8 in (22.22 mm) diameter: 9 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.11111 in (2.822 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 7/8-14 UNF and 7/8-20 UNEF.

  • Tap drill49/64"76% thread, 19.45 mm
  • Pitch9 TPI2.822 mm
  • Major diameter0.8750 in22.225 mm
  • Pitch diameter0.8028 in20.392 mm
  • Minor diameter (nut)0.7547 in19.170 mm
  • Tensile stress area0.462 in²298 mm²
  • Clearance hole15/16 innormal fit, ASME B18.2.8

7/8-9 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0019 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

7/8-9 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 7/8-9 UNC: 49/64" (0.7656 in, 19.45 mm)

This leaves about 76% thread in the tapped hole.

49/64" is the drill listed for 7/8-9 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.1094 in under the 0.8750 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.755 and 0.778 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 7/8-9 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 7/8-9 UNC. The nearest is M22×2.5, which is 0.22 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 2.500 mm against 2.822 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 7/8-9 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.11111 in = 1 ÷ 9 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0962 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.8750 − 0.0722 = 0.8028 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.8750 − 0.1203 = 0.7547 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.8750 − 0.1323 = 0.7427 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.7667² = 0.46173 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.52°
