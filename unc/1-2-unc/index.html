1/2-13 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

1/2-13 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 1/2 in (12.70 mm) diameter: 13 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.07692 in (1.954 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 1/2-20 UNF and 1/2-28 UNEF.

1/2-13 is a standard size for structural, machinery and anchor bolts.

1/2-13 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0015 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

1/2-13 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 1/2-13 UNC: 27/64" (0.4219 in, 10.72 mm)

This leaves about 78% thread in the tapped hole.

27/64" is the drill listed for 1/2-13 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0781 in under the 0.5000 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.417 and 0.434 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 1/2-13 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 1/2-13 UNC. The nearest is M12×1.75, which is 0.70 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 1.750 mm against 1.954 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 1/2-13 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.07692 in = 1 ÷ 13 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0666 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.5000 − 0.0500 = 0.4500 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.5000 − 0.0833 = 0.4167 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.5000 − 0.0916 = 0.4084 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.4251² = 0.14190 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 3.11°
