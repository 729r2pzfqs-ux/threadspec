9/16-12 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

9/16-12 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 9/16 in (14.29 mm) diameter: 12 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.08333 in (2.117 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 9/16-18 UNF and 9/16-24 UNEF.

  • Pitch diameter0.5084 in12.913 mm

9/16-12 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0016 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

9/16-12 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 9/16-12 UNC: 31/64" (0.4844 in, 12.30 mm)

This leaves about 72% thread in the tapped hole.

31/64" is the drill listed for 9/16-12 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0781 in under the 0.5625 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.472 and 0.490 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 9/16-12 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

Clearance hole for 9/16 in

ASME B18.2.8 does not list the 9/16 in size, so there is no standard clearance hole for the 9/16 in size. Size the hole from the fit the joint needs.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 9/16-12 UNC. The nearest is M14×2, which is 0.29 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 2.000 mm against 2.117 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 9/16-12 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.08333 in = 1 ÷ 12 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0722 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.5625 − 0.0541 = 0.5084 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.5625 − 0.0902 = 0.4723 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.5625 − 0.0992 = 0.4633 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.4813² = 0.18194 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.99°
