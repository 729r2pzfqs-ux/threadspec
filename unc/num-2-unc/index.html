#2-56 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

#2-56 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the number 2 size (major diameter 0.086 in, 2.18 mm): 56 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.01786 in (0.454 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are #2-64 UNF.

  • Tap drill#5069% thread, 1.78 mm
  • Pitch56 TPI0.454 mm
  • Major diameter0.0860 in2.184 mm
  • Pitch diameter0.0744 in1.890 mm
  • Minor diameter (nut)0.0667 in1.693 mm
  • Tensile stress area0.00370 in²2.39 mm²

#2-56 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0006 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

#2-56 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for #2-56 UNC: #50 (0.0700 in, 1.78 mm)

This leaves about 69% thread in the tapped hole.

#50 is the drill listed for #2-56 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.0160 in under the 0.0860 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.067 and 0.074 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for #2-56 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

#2-56 UNC strength

SAE J429 starts at 1/4 in, so the SAE grades give no proof load for #2-56 UNC. For an estimate, multiply the tensile stress area of 0.00370 in² by the strength of the material: for example, 60,000 psi gives 222 lbf.

#2 pitches compared

All standard pitches on the #2 diameter. A finer pitch gives a shallower thread, a larger core and a smaller lead angle; a coarser pitch is faster to assemble and more tolerant of damage and plating.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

How the #2-56 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.01786 in = 1 ÷ 56 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.0155 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 0.0860 − 0.0116 = 0.0744 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 0.0860 − 0.0193 = 0.0667 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 0.0860 − 0.0213 = 0.0647 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.0686² = 0.00370 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 4.37°
