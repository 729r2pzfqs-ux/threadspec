1-8 UNC Thread Dimensions and Tap Drill

1-8 UNC is the Unified coarse thread for the 1 in (25.40 mm) diameter: 8 threads per inch, a pitch of 0.12500 in (3.175 mm), on a 60° profile. ASME B1.1 lists it in the UNC series. Finer threads on the same diameter are 1-12 UNF and 1-20 UNEF.

  • Pitch8 TPI3.175 mm

  • Pitch diameter0.9188 in23.338 mm
  • Minor diameter (nut)0.8647 in21.963 mm

1-8 UNC limits of size (class 2A bolt, 2B nut)

Classes 2A and 2B are the general-purpose fits for commercial bolts, screws and nuts. The 2A external thread has an allowance of 0.0020 in, so its maximum size is that much below basic; the 2B nut starts at the basic size.

1-8 UNC tap drill size

Tap drill for 1-8 UNC: 7/8" (0.8750 in, 22.22 mm)

This leaves about 77% thread in the tapped hole.

7/8" is the drill listed for 1-8 UNC in standard tap drill charts. It is 0.1250 in under the 1.0000 in major diameter.

The 2B minor diameter of the finished nut thread must lie between 0.865 and 0.890 in. The recommended drill falls inside that band.

Other drills that work for 1-8 UNC

A larger drill cuts less thread and taps more easily; a smaller one gives a fuller thread but loads the tap harder.

When to choose a coarse or a fine thread

Closest metric thread

No metric thread is interchangeable with 1-8 UNC. The nearest is M24×3, which is 1.40 mm smaller in diameter with a pitch of 3.000 mm against 3.175 mm. A nut may start on the wrong thread and then bind or strip, so identify by both diameter and pitch.

How the 1-8 UNC dimensions are calculated

Every basic dimension follows from the major diameter and the pitch:

  • P = 0.12500 in = 1 ÷ 8 threads per inch
  • H = 0.866025 × P = 0.1083 in
  • d2 = d − 0.649519 × P = 1.0000 − 0.0812 = 0.9188 in
  • D1 = d − 1.082532 × P = 1.0000 − 0.1353 = 0.8647 in
  • d3 = d − 1.190785 × P = 1.0000 − 0.1488 = 0.8512 in (external root, 11H/16 deep)
  • As = 0.7854 × (d − 0.9743 × P)² = 0.7854 × 0.8782² = 0.60575 in²
  • Lead angle = arctan(P ÷ (π × d2)) = 2.48°
